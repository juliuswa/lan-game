Coin Rush

Move with WASD / arrow keys. Coins spawn around the wandering spawner — push it around. Slamming into a wall costs a coin. Hold Space to aim at the mouse (you drift slowly), release to dash and shove players — recharges after 3 coins. Slam the goldpot into the arena edge and it breaks, reappearing elsewhere 3s later. Black holes teleport you to a random spot. The goldpot drops traps — type the shown W/A/S/D combination in order to break free (a mistake restarts it). Press Ä for 5 bonus coins. First to 20 coins wins the round, first to 5 rounds wins the match. After each round the lowest scorer picks a power-up (A/D or W/S to choose, Space to confirm).

Coin Rush

Grab coins around the wandering goldpot, shove your friends into walls and black holes, and be first to 50 coins. First to 5 rounds wins the match.

Scores

    How to play

    WASD move · Space dash toward the mouse (recharges after 3 coins) and ready up between rounds. M toggles sound.

    The goldpot spills 2 coins every second — slam it into the arena edge to break it and stop the flow. Walls cost 5 coins on a hard hit. Black holes teleport you; red ones also cost 5 coins. Traps lock you until you type the shown W/A/S/D combination.

    Chaos gems: ⇄ swap places, $ swap coins, 🔒 trap everyone else. The round's lowest scorer picks a power-up.
